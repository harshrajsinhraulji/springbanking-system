# Code of Conduct

We are committed to fostering a welcoming and inclusive community. By participating, you agree to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Use inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, or any form of aggression. Report violations to the maintainers via issues or email listed in the README.

Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent bans.
