How Project Adhikaar Works

We make it easy to understand and verify your account status.

1. Learn

Use our simple guides and chatbot, Aadhaar Dost, to learn the difference between a linked and a DBT-seeded account.

2. Check

Use our 1-Click tool to instantly check if your bank account is correctly seeded for DBT payments.

3. Resolve

If there's an issue, we'll guide you on the next steps and help you find a local DBT-Sathi for in-person assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

A 'linked' account uses Aadhaar for identity proof (KYC). A 'seeded' account is specifically approved to receive government money (like scholarships) through the Aadhaar payment system managed by NPCI. You MUST seed your account.
You only need your Aadhaar card and your bank passbook. Visit your bank branch and ask for the 'Aadhaar Seeding and DBT Consent Form'. Fill it out and submit it.
No, there is absolutely no cost for Aadhaar seeding. It is a free service provided by all banks as per government guidelines.
After you submit the form at the bank, it usually takes 3 to 5 working days for the seeding to be completed and reflected in the NPCI system.

Check DBT Seeding Status

For your security, this check must be done on the official Government of India (UIDAI) website. We will guide you there.

Instructions:

  1. Click the button below to go to the secure UIDAI portal.
  2. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar Number and the security captcha.
  3. Click 'Send OTP'. You will receive an OTP on the mobile number registered with your Aadhaar.
  4. Enter the OTP to see your bank seeding status.
